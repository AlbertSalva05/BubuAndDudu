        Dear Dudu,

        I started writing this about six times. Every version sounded like a greeting card and you would've laughed at me, so here's the real one.

        These 1 year and 9 months haven't been perfect. We've had fights that went on way longer than they needed to, "I'm fine"s that were absolutely not fine, and nights where we were both too stubborn to be the first one to say sorry. If our relationship were a ride, it would have a sign at the entrance warning people with weak hearts.

        But that ride also has the parts I'd queue for again and again. Laughing until we couldn't breathe. Your random messages in the middle of my day. The cuddles that somehow fix everything. The quiet moments where neither of us has to say anything at all.

        And somewhere along the way, we built a whole little household. Chicolat, Patrick, Jiso and Ora, who have honestly seen far too much. And Chuckie, the only innocent one among us, who loves us both even on the days we're being ridiculous.

        What I love most is that we're still here. Not because it's always easy — but because every time it gets hard, we keep choosing each other. I don't take that lightly. I know I'm not always easy to love, and you do it anyway.

        I'm excited for whatever's next. New chapters, new places, probably new arguments, definitely more making up. As long as it's with you, I'm in.

        Happy 1 year and 9 months, Dudu.

        I'd choose you again.
        Even knowing how chaotic you are.
